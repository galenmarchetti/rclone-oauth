Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This OAuth client ("rclone") is private and single-user. Only the application owner uses it. It is not a public product.

What data it accesses

The app accesses only the application owner's own Google Drive files. It uses this access only to sync and copy those files with rclone, which runs on the owner's own devices.

How data is handled

No data is sent to any third-party server. The data is not sold or shared. It is not used for advertising or AI training.

Authentication credentials are stored locally on the owner's devices and are not shared with third parties.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Contact the application owner through the support email shown on this app's Google sign-in consent screen.
